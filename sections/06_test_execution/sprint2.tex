\subsubsection*{Skipped tests}

The 17 skipped tests need PostgreSQL.
CI runs the backend on SQLite, which cannot open two concurrent connections, add \texttt{CHECK} constraints or enforce decimal scale, so these tests skip themselves.
They cover invitation concurrency, wallet locking and schema constraints of the organization features.
Their behavior is not covered by the evidence in this report.
